TasteTrails Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

TasteTrails helps you find restaurants and menu items. This page is the public copy you can host and paste into App Store Connect.

Account

If you create an account, the app stores your name, email, and a password hash so you can sign in. You can delete the account inside the app from Settings, Account, Delete Account Permanently. Deletion removes the server account when that request succeeds, and clears profile, ratings, and preferences stored on the device.

Location

Location is used only after you tap Use my location, to sort and filter menu items by distance. Until then, search uses a Charlotte, NC starting point. The app does not track location in the background.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are optional. They are used when you add a profile photo or a photo of a dish.

Preferences

Dietary choices, theme, and notification settings are saved on the device. Dietary labels are estimates from menu text, not a guarantee from the kitchen.

Social

If you follow someone, send a follow request, approve or decline a request, remove a follower, unfollow, block an account, or report an account, TasteTrails stores that relationship with your account. Posts, ratings, lists, and activity you add are stored with your account. A public profile can be seen by other people. A private profile shows only your name and photo until you approve a follower; posts, ratings, lists, activity, and follower lists stay hidden from everyone else. Blocking hides both accounts from each other’s search and stops follow requests. Reports are stored so user-generated content can be reviewed. Deleting your account removes these records from the server.

Sharing

TasteTrails does not sell personal information and does not use advertising tracking.
