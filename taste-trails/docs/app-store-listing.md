# TasteTrails App Store listing

**Name:** TasteTrails

**Subtitle:** Discover Food Worth Remembering.

**Promotional text:** Find what to eat, where to go, and which dishes are actually worth ordering.

**Description:**

TasteTrails is a social guide to food, not just restaurants. Search a dish, compare the places that serve it, and open the restaurant’s own menu before you go.

Discover meals people recommend. Explore sit-down restaurants in Charlotte, Fort Mill, and Greenwood, or wherever you share a location. Rate individual dishes from 1 to 10, add a photo, and save places you want to try or have already visited.

Menus show the sections, descriptions, and prices the restaurant published. Community scores appear only after someone rates that dish, and a food score keeps a single review from outranking a place with more ratings. Follow friends, keep an account private, and share recommendations in groups.

TasteTrails does not book your table and does not invent dishes, prices, or reviews. Confirm hours and dietary needs with the restaurant.

**What’s not in this version:** sponsored dishes, promotions, reservation booking, and restaurant analytics.
